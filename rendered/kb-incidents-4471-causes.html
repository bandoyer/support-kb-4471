The parser bug

The shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant's dates come out flipped: 7 April becomes 4 July. It reaches every tenant on file, through the app, through email, and through the booking-v2 form. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

The hint bug

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at a day-first tenant type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant's way. It reaches only reports that come through the form at a tenant on booking-v3. The same tenants' app and email reports belong to the parser bug.

The fix is a different one: the form's hint has to match the order the form reads.

How to tell them apart

Use causeOf in causes.ts. It looks at the tenant's locale, the tenant's form and the channel.

  • Not the subject. Every subject on a hint report also appears on parser reports.
  • Not the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter's browser. The "tagged as" column shows how often that guess disagrees with the cause.
  • Not the raw export. Its pages overlap and some reports come twice. The table counts each id once.

Reports that do not read as a flip

The note under the table counts reports the desk read as a one-day move. The kb files a report by its tenant, so they count under the parser bug. A month-first parser cannot move 12 May to 11 May, though, and R-41344 says clients in Lisbon and Toronto got different days for one meeting. That points at time zones. Nobody has checked it. If engineering finds another cause, they move out of the parser count and get their own ticket.
